New Employee Onboarding Guide (Sample)

Week 1: Setup and Orientation
On your first day, IT will provision your laptop and accounts before you
arrive. You will meet with your manager for a 1:1 to review your 30-60-90
day goals, and complete mandatory compliance training covering security,
data privacy, and code of conduct. All new hires must finish compliance
training within their first five business days.

Week 2: Team Integration
You will be paired with an onboarding buddy, an experienced teammate who
answers day-to-day questions for your first 90 days. You will also get
read access to the internal documentation wiki and be added to your team's
project boards.

Equipment and Software
New hires receive a laptop, monitor, and any specialized hardware relevant
to their role. Standard software includes the company's chat tool, email,
calendar, and the internal RAG assistant used for searching company
documentation. IT support tickets can be filed through the helpdesk portal
and are typically resolved within one business day.

Probation Period
All new employees have a 90-day probationary period during which either
the employee or the company may end employment without notice. A formal
performance review is conducted at the end of the probation period.
