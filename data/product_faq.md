# Acme Widget Pro - Frequently Asked Questions (Sample)

## What is Acme Widget Pro?

Acme Widget Pro is a desktop productivity application that helps teams
track tasks, share documents, and run video meetings from a single
workspace. It is available on Windows, macOS, and Linux.

## What subscription plans are available?

There are three plans: Starter (free, up to 3 users), Team ($12 per user
per month, up to 50 users), and Enterprise (custom pricing, unlimited
users with single sign-on and dedicated support). All paid plans include a
14-day free trial with no credit card required.

## How do I cancel my subscription?

You can cancel anytime from Settings > Billing > Cancel Subscription.
Cancelling stops future charges immediately, but you keep access to paid
features until the end of the current billing period. There are no
cancellation fees.

## Is my data encrypted?

Yes. All data is encrypted in transit using TLS 1.3 and at rest using
AES-256. Enterprise customers can additionally configure their own
encryption keys through a bring-your-own-key (BYOK) integration.

## Does Acme Widget Pro support integrations?

Yes. Acme Widget Pro integrates with Slack, Google Calendar, GitHub, and
Zapier out of the box. Enterprise plans also get access to a REST API and
webhooks for building custom integrations.
