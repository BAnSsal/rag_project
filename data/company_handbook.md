# Acme Co. Employee Handbook (Sample)

## Remote Work Policy

Employees may work remotely up to four days per week. Everyone is expected
to be in the office on Wednesdays for the weekly team sync. Remote work
requests for more than four days per week must be approved by a manager and
by HR at least two weeks in advance.

## Vacation Policy

Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid vacation per year, accrued
monthly at a rate of 1.67 days per month. Unused vacation days roll over up
to a maximum of 10 days into the next calendar year; anything beyond that
cap is forfeited. Vacation requests should be submitted through the HR
portal at least one week before the requested start date.

## Refund Policy

Customers may request a full refund within 30 days of purchase, no
questions asked. Refund requests after 30 days but within 90 days are
handled on a case-by-case basis by the support team. After 90 days, refunds
are not offered, but store credit may be issued at the discretion of a
support manager.

## Expense Reimbursement

Employees can submit expense reports for approved business expenses,
including travel, client meals, and conference tickets. Reports must be
submitted within 60 days of the expense and require a manager's approval
for any single item over $500. Reimbursements are processed within 10
business days of approval.
